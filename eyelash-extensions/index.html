Eyelash Extensions

Length and Volume Without Daily Mascara

Eyelash extensions in Jacksonville for sparse lashes and mornings with no time for eye makeup.

Contact Us Today

Sonia’s Sculpting & Beyond applies eyelash extensions for clients in Jacksonville who want to wake up with full, defined lashes that require no mascara, curling, or daily maintenance. If your natural lashes are short, thin, or straight, extensions add length and curl that frame your eyes and remain intact through showers, workouts, and sleep. Each synthetic lash is bonded to an individual natural lash using medical-grade adhesive, creating a customized look that can be as subtle or dramatic as you prefer.

The application takes place while you lie with your eyes closed on a cushioned table. A technician isolates one natural lash at a time using fine-tipped tweezers, then dips a single extension into adhesive and attaches it near the base of the natural lash without touching the skin. Length, curl, and thickness are varied to match your eye shape and the overall style you request. A full set can take up to two hours, and the results last several weeks as long as you avoid rubbing your eyes or using oil-based cleansers that break down the adhesive bond.

How Extensions Are Applied and What Maintenance Involves

You arrive with clean lashes, free of any makeup or oil residue. The technician places a gel pad under your lower lashes to protect the skin and keep your eyes comfortably closed. A small amount of adhesive is used on each extension; the bond sets within seconds, but full cure time takes several hours, so you avoid water contact for at least twenty-four hours after the appointment.

Once the extensions are in place, your eyes look noticeably more open and awake without any additional makeup. The lashes hold their curl and length through humidity, and you no longer see clumping, flaking, or raccoon eyes at the end of the day. We use lightweight synthetic fibers that do not weigh down your natural lashes, and the adhesive is tested for safe use near the sensitive eye area.

Extensions shed naturally as your own lashes go through their growth cycle, which means you will lose a few each week. Fill appointments every two to three weeks replace lost extensions and maintain fullness. Clean your lashes daily with a gentle, oil-free cleanser and a soft brush. Avoid waterproof mascara, lash curlers, and sleeping face-down, all of which can damage or dislodge extensions prematurely.

Common Questions About Lash Extension Care and Results

What does the application process feel like?

You lie still with your eyes closed and should not feel pain, though some people notice a faint adhesive smell during the session.

How long do eyelash extensions last?

A full set lasts four to six weeks with proper care, but fills are needed every two to three weeks to maintain consistent volume as natural lashes shed.

Why do extensions eliminate the need for mascara?

The synthetic fibers are already dark, curled, and coated, so they provide the same visual effect mascara aims for without any application.

When should you avoid lash extensions?

If you have chronic eye infections, very weak natural lashes, or allergies to adhesive ingredients, extensions may not be suitable for you.

Can you swim or exercise with extensions?

Yes — once the adhesive has fully cured, extensions are water-resistant and hold up through sweat, showers, and swimming.
