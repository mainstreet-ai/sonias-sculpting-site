Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most about our treatments and services.

What's the difference between body sculpting and body contouring?

Body sculpting focuses on enhancing, toning, and tightening specific areas like the stomach, arms, and legs using muscle stimulation techniques like EM-Sculpt. Body contouring targets stubborn fat and cellulite using technologies like cavitation, radio frequency, laser lipo, and wood therapy. At Sonia's Sculpting & Beyond, both services can be combined for comprehensive results.

How does non-invasive body sculpting work?

Non-invasive body sculpting uses advanced technologies like EM-Sculpt muscle stimulation to tone and tighten targeted areas without surgery or downtime. The treatment stimulates muscle contractions to build strength and definition while tightening skin. It's effective for areas like the stomach, arms, legs, buttocks, and breasts.

What treatments help reduce cellulite effectively?

Cellulite reduction combines multiple modalities including vacuum therapy, radio frequency, and specialized massage techniques to smooth skin texture. These treatments work by improving circulation, breaking down fat deposits, and stimulating collagen production. We tailor combinations based on your specific cellulite concerns and skin type.

When should you get a post-op lymphatic massage?

Post-op lymphatic massage should begin soon after surgical procedures, typically within days to weeks as recommended by your surgeon. This specialized massage reduces swelling, promotes healing, and helps prevent fluid buildup. It's especially beneficial after cosmetic or body contouring surgeries to enhance recovery and results.

Does radio frequency skin tightening work in Florida's humidity?

Yes, radio frequency skin tightening is effective in Jacksonville's humid climate and can actually benefit from proper hydration levels. The treatment stimulates collagen production to improve skin elasticity and reduce sagging regardless of weather conditions. Consistent hydration helps optimize and maintain your results.

What's the difference between microdermabrasion and hydrodermabrasion facials?

Microdermabrasion uses gentle exfoliation to remove dead skin cells and improve texture, while hydrodermabrasion combines exfoliation with hydrating serums for deeper cleansing and moisture. Hydrodermabrasion is gentler and more hydrating, making it ideal for sensitive or dry skin. Both treatments brighten your complexion and can be customized to your skin's needs.

How does Lipo 360 body sculpting target multiple areas?

Lipo 360 is a comprehensive approach that targets the entire midsection including abdomen, flanks, and back for balanced, 360-degree contouring. It combines multiple non-invasive technologies to reduce fat, tighten skin, and create smooth, proportional results.

Can laser hair removal work on all skin tones?

Advanced IPL laser hair removal technology can safely treat various skin tones when properly calibrated. The treatment targets hair follicles for permanent hair reduction on multiple body areas. We adjust settings based on your specific skin type and hair color for safe, effective results.

What does wood therapy do for body contouring?

Wood therapy uses specially designed wooden tools to massage and sculpt the body, breaking down fat deposits and improving circulation. This technique helps reduce stubborn fat, smooth cellulite, and contour areas like thighs, abdomen, and buttocks. It's often combined with other modalities like cavitation or radio frequency for enhanced results.

How does red light therapy promote hair growth?

Red light therapy stimulates hair follicles at the cellular level, increasing blood flow and promoting natural hair growth cycles. The treatment uses specific wavelengths to energize cells and combat hair loss without surgery or medications. It's effective for both men and women experiencing thinning hair or pattern baldness.

What should teens expect from a teen facial treatment?

Teen facials address common adolescent skin concerns like acne, oiliness, and congestion using gentle, age-appropriate products and techniques. The treatment includes cleansing, exfoliation, extractions if needed, and education about proper skincare routines. It's designed to be comfortable and educational while promoting healthy skin habits early.

What's the biggest mistake people make with cavitation treatments?

The biggest mistake is not drinking enough water before and after cavitation sessions. Cavitation breaks down fat cells, and proper hydration is essential for your body to naturally flush out the released fat. We emphasize hydration and provide guidelines to maximize your body contouring results.
