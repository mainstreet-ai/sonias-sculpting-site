Questions About Nail Health and Service Options

What is the difference between regular and gel polish?

Gel polish cures under UV or LED light and lasts up to two weeks without chipping, while regular polish air-dries and typically lasts five to seven days.

How can you keep your nails strong between appointments?

Moisturize your cuticles daily, avoid using your nails as tools, and wear gloves when cleaning or doing tasks that expose your hands to water and chemicals.

Why do cuticles need regular care?

Overgrown cuticles trap dirt and bacteria, create uneven polish lines, and can lead to hangnails or infections if not managed properly.

When should you remove gel polish?

Gel polish should be soaked off after two to three weeks to avoid lifting and moisture getting trapped beneath the polish, which can weaken the nail.

Can nail services help repair damage?

Yes — regular filing, buffing, and strengthening treatments gradually improve the appearance and resilience of damaged nails.
